OmniCorp Travel and Expense Policy
All business travel must be booked through the OmniTravel portal at least 14 days in advance.
Daily meal allowance is capped at 75 USD domestic and 110 USD international.
Business class flights are permitted only for flights exceeding 6 hours.
All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of trip completion with itemized receipts.
Expenses above 500 USD require pre-approval from a department director.
